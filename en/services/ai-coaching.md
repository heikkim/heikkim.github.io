<!-- 2. The core challenge -->
<section id="the-challenge">
    <h2>The Challenge</h2>
    <p>
      AI keeps moving faster than the people who are supposed to lead it. Some in your team may have already adopted it.
      You might still find yourself trying to keep up with a chat window and twenty open tabs. <strong>The result is quiet and expensive:</strong> no
      real delegation of work to AI, no AI for the board presentation, and no leverage from the tools you are paying for.
    </p>
    <p>
      Generic training will not close the gap. A two-hour webinar on prompting gives you tips. It does not give you
      the workflow, the prompt library, or the agent that handles the forty minutes of inbox de-cluttering you do
      every Monday morning.
    </p>
    <p>
      At the same time you've a business to run. You need the efficiency boost.
    </p>
</section>

<!-- 3. The approach and solution -->
<section id="the-solution">
    <h2>The Solution</h2>
    <p>
      Getting down the the business of learning new skills is the only way out. Trial and error gets you forward, but
      the pace is not necessarily satisfying. Focusing on automating your work, what moves the needle, and gives you that extra 2 hours per week
      to think about next year's strategy is what you should be looking at. Not for a demo, not for a seat in a webinar,
      unless you really have the time for it.
    </p>
    <p>
      Tap directly into expertise of others. The AI Coaching service here, is a one-to-one coaching on your actual work.
    </p>
    <p>
      We start with your work week. In the first session we map the recurring, high-value work
      in your day, e.g. the board presentation, customer escalations, team/unit reports, the inbox. Then we begin
      with the tasks that create the most value first.
    </p>
    <p>
      <strong>Every session is hands-on:</strong> you drive, I coach. We build the prompts, the workflows and the automations
      live, on your screen. You keep everything we build, including the parts that work exactly for you, because you made them.
    </p>
</section>
