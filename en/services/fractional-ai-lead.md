<!-- 2. The core challenge -->
<section id="the-challenge">
    <h2>The Challenge</h2>
    <p>
      You already know AI matters. Maybe somebody has already run the pilots, bought the licences and started the
      experiments. But you're not seeing AI scaling across the company. Some of the pilots you've started are silently disappearing. Old ways of doing are kicking in.
    </p>
    <p>
      The bottleneck is seldom AI tooling, because those tools are abundant. <strong>It is bandwidth</strong>. Every serious AI decision around data readiness, vendor
      selection, security, compliance or change management needs senior leadership. A senior leader whose table is already full does
      not have the free capacity to make those calls. Meanwhile the rest of the organisation is drowning in tools, each one promising to change this and that.
    </p>
    <p>
      <strong>That leaves two suboptimal choices:</strong> Hire a full-time AI executive before the business case is proven, or keep
      stitching pilots together and hope scale happens on its own. Both cost you time, and both cost you
      credibility internally.
    </p>
</section>

<!-- 3. The approach and solution -->
<section id="the-solution">
    <h2>The Solution</h2>
    <p>
      A senior leader who has the expertise, bandwith, and the capacity, to take decisions and steer actions across the company is the right call. Having a seat in the management team is a must, otherwise the change can lose momentum due to inadequate management attention and care.
    </p>
    <p>
      The engagement needs to be hands-on, not plain advisory or high level strategy. The hard chores of identifying AI champions & nurturing them, evaluating vendors, creating governance policy, facilitating workshops, running the pilots, <strong>and putting the first business automations into production so that it sticks</strong> need proper attention. After all, the question is about competitive capacity of the company!
    </p>
    <p>
      The rest is about giving a mandate to act, while staying informed through appropriate control points. The Fractional AI Lead model is a low-threshold, risk-free approach for solving the challenge.
    </p>
</section>
